Subhanaka	Recited at the start of the prayer, in the first rak'ah	Subhanakallahumma wa bihamdika wa tabarakasmuka wa ta'ala jadduka wa la ilaha ghayruk.	O Allah! I declare You free of all imperfection and praise You. Blessed is Your name and exalted is Your majesty. There is no god but You.	
A'udhu – Basmala	When starting to read the Quran	A'udhu billahi minash-shaytanir-rajim. Bismillahir-rahmanir-rahim.	I seek refuge in Allah from the accursed Satan. In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.	
At-Tahiyyat	Recited in every sitting of the prayer	At-tahiyyatu lillahi was-salawatu wat-tayyibat. As-salamu alayka ayyuhan-nabiyyu wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh. As-salamu alayna wa ala ibadillahis-salihin. Ash-hadu an la ilaha illallah wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluh.	All greetings, prayers and good words are for Allah. Peace be upon you, O Prophet, and the mercy and blessings of Allah. Peace be upon us and upon the righteous servants of Allah. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and messenger.	Bukhari, Muslim
Allahumma Salli	In the last sitting, after At-Tahiyyat	Allahumma salli ala Muhammadin wa ala ali Muhammad. Kama sallayta ala Ibrahima wa ala ali Ibrahim. Innaka hamidun majid.	O Allah! Send Your mercy upon Muhammad and his family as You sent it upon Ibrahim and his family. Truly You are Praiseworthy and Glorious.	Bukhari, Muslim
Allahumma Barik	After Allahumma Salli	Allahumma barik ala Muhammadin wa ala ali Muhammad. Kama barakta ala Ibrahima wa ala ali Ibrahim. Innaka hamidun majid.	O Allah! Bless Muhammad and his family as You blessed Ibrahim and his family. Truly You are Praiseworthy and Glorious.	Bukhari, Muslim
Rabbana Atina	In the last sitting, before the salam	Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina adhaban-nar.	Our Lord! Give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.	al-Baqarah, 201
Rabbanaghfir li	After Rabbana Atina	Rabbanaghfir li wa li-walidayya wa lil-mu'minina yawma yaqumul-hisab.	Our Lord! Forgive me, my parents and all the believers on the Day of Reckoning.	Ibrahim, 41
Qunut Prayer – 1	In the third rak'ah of the witr prayer	Allahumma inna nasta'inuka wa nastaghfiruka wa nastahdik. Wa nu'minu bika wa natubu ilayk. Wa natawakkalu alayka wa nuthni alaykal-khayra kullahu nashkuruk. Wa la nakfuruka wa nakhla'u wa natruku man yafjuruk.	O Allah! We seek Your help, ask Your forgiveness and ask You to guide us. We believe in You, repent to You and trust in You. We praise You with all good. We thank You and do not deny Your blessings. We abandon and break away from whoever defies You.	
Qunut Prayer – 2	After the first Qunut prayer	Allahumma iyyaka na'budu wa laka nusalli wa nasjudu wa ilayka nas'a wa nahfidu narju rahmataka wa nakhsha adhabaka inna adhabaka bil-kuffari mulhiq.	O Allah! You alone we worship; for You alone we pray and prostrate. To You we hasten and turn. We hope for Your mercy and fear Your punishment. Surely Your punishment will reach the disbelievers.	
Amantu	The foundations of faith	Amantu billahi wa mala'ikatihi wa kutubihi wa rusulihi wal-yawmil-akhiri wa bil-qadari khayrihi wa sharrihi minallahi ta'ala wal-ba'thu ba'dal-mawti haqq. Ash-hadu an la ilaha illallah wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluh.	I believe in Allah, His angels, His books, His messengers, the Last Day, and in destiny, that good and evil are from Allah. Resurrection after death is true. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah and that Muhammad is His servant and messenger.	
Kalimat at-Tawhid	The essence of Islam	La ilaha illallah Muhammadur rasulullah.	There is no god but Allah; Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.	
Kalimat ash-Shahada	The first condition of being a Muslim	Ash-hadu an la ilaha illallah wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluh.	I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and messenger.	
Before eating	When sitting down to eat	Bismillah. (If we forget at the start: Bismillahi awwalahu wa akhirah.)	In the name of Allah. (In the name of Allah, at its beginning and its end.)	Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi
After eating	When getting up from the meal	Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi at'amana wa saqana wa ja'alana muslimin.	Praise be to Allah who fed us, gave us drink and made us Muslims.	Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi
Before sleeping	When getting into bed	Bismikallahumma amutu wa ahya.	O Allah! In Your name I die (sleep) and live (wake).	Bukhari
On waking up	When we open our eyes in the morning	Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi ahyana ba'da ma amatana wa ilayhin-nushur.	Praise be to Allah who gave us life (woke us) after He had caused us to die (sleep). To Him is the return.	Bukhari
Leaving the house	When going out of the door	Bismillahi tawakkaltu alallah wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah.	In the name of Allah I go out; I have put my trust in Allah. There is no power and no strength except with Allah.	Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi
Entering the toilet	Said before entering	Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-khubuthi wal-khaba'ith.	O Allah! I seek refuge in You from all filth and evil.	Bukhari, Muslim
Leaving the toilet	Said on coming out	Ghufranak.	O Allah! I ask Your forgiveness.	Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi
Supplication after the adhan	After the adhan ends	Allahumma rabba hadhihid-da'watit-tammati was-salatil-qa'imati ati Muhammadanil-wasilata wal-fadilata wab'ath-hu maqamam mahmudanil-ladhi wa'adtah.	O Allah, Lord of this perfect call and the prayer about to be established! Grant Muhammad the wasilah and virtue, and raise him to the praised station You have promised him.	Bukhari
Setting out on a journey	When getting into a vehicle	Subhanal-ladhi sakhkhara lana hadha wa ma kunna lahu muqrinin. Wa inna ila rabbina lamunqalibun.	Glory be to Him who has placed this at our service, for we could never have done it ourselves. And surely to our Lord we shall return.	az-Zukhruf, 13-14
While studying	When starting to learn	Rabbi zidni ilma.	My Lord! Increase me in knowledge.	Ta-Ha, 114
Starting a difficult task	Before an exam or a task	Rabbi yassir wa la tu'assir, Rabbi tammim bil-khayr.	My Lord! Make it easy, do not make it hard. My Lord! Complete it with goodness.	
For parents	Can be recited at any time	Rabbirhamhuma kama rabbayani saghira.	My Lord! Have mercy on them as they lovingly raised me when I was small.	al-Isra, 24
When sneezing	What do the sneezer and the listener say?	Sneezer: Alhamdulillah. Listener: Yarhamukallah. Sneezer replies: Yahdikumullah.	Praise be to Allah. – May Allah have mercy on you. – May Allah guide you.	Bukhari
