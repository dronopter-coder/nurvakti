How many daily prayers are obligatory?	Five: Fajr (dawn), Dhuhr (noon), Asr (afternoon), Maghrib (sunset) and Isha (night).
What are the obligatory acts of wudu (ablution)?	Washing the face, washing the arms up to and including the elbows, wiping a quarter of the head, and washing the feet up to and including the ankles.
What are the conditions of prayer?	The six external conditions are: purity from ritual impurity, purity from filth, covering the awrah, facing the qibla, the prayer time, and intention.
When is tayammum performed?	When no water can be found, or when using water would cause harm such as illness; it is done with clean earth.
When are we considered travellers?	According to the Diyanet, a journey of about 90 km or more makes one a traveller; four-rak'ah obligatory prayers are then shortened to two.
What is the nisab for zakat?	If, beyond basic needs and debts, one has owned wealth worth 80.18 g of gold or 561 g of silver for one year, one fortieth (2.5%) of it is given as zakat.
What breaks the fast?	Deliberately eating, drinking or having marital relations. Eating or drinking out of forgetfulness does not break the fast.
For whom is the Friday prayer obligatory?	For men who are sane, mature, free, resident and healthy.
When is ghusl (full ablution) required?	Ghusl is required after janabah (major impurity), menstruation and postnatal bleeding.
At which times is prayer disliked (makruh)?	At sunrise, when the sun is at its zenith, and when it reddens shortly before sunset.
How many pillars does Islam have?	Five: the declaration of faith (shahada), prayer, zakat, fasting in Ramadan, and pilgrimage for those who are able.
What are the articles of faith?	Six: belief in Allah, His angels, His books, His prophets, the Last Day, and that good and evil come from Allah (divine decree).
What is the difference between fard and wajib?	Fard is established by definitive proof; one who denies it leaves Islam. Wajib is established by somewhat weaker proof; one who neglects it sins, but one who denies it does not leave Islam. (Hanafi)
What invalidates wudu?	Anything exiting from the front or back passage, blood or pus flowing from the body, vomiting a mouthful, fainting, intoxication and sleeping lying down.
Does touching a woman invalidate wudu?	According to the Hanafi school, it does not.
What are the obligatory acts of ghusl?	Rinsing the mouth, rinsing the nose and washing the entire body. (Hanafi)
How long may one wipe over leather socks (khuff)?	One day and night for a resident, three days and nights for a traveller.
How many obligatory elements does the prayer have?	Twelve. Six are outside the prayer (the conditions). Six are within it: the opening takbir, standing, recitation, bowing, prostration and the final sitting.
What invalidates the prayer?	Speaking, eating or drinking, turning away from the qibla, laughing aloud, losing wudu and exposing the awrah.
When is the prostration of forgetfulness (sajdah sahw) performed?	When a wajib act of the prayer is forgotten or done out of place. In the final sitting, after the tashahhud, one gives salam to the right, makes two prostrations and then completes the prayer.
How many rak'ahs is the witr prayer?	Three rak'ahs, and it is wajib. It is prayed after Isha.
What is the tarawih prayer?	A sunnah prayer of twenty rak'ahs prayed after Isha on the nights of Ramadan. (Diyanet)
How is the funeral prayer performed?	It is a communal obligation (fard kifayah). It is prayed standing, without bowing or prostration, with four takbirs.
How does a sick person pray?	One who cannot stand prays sitting; one who cannot sit prays lying down; if that is not possible, by gestures.
What is required if a Ramadan fast is deliberately broken?	Making it up (qada) is required. If it was broken by deliberate eating, drinking or marital relations, expiation (kaffarah) is also required: 60 consecutive days of fasting.
What is fidyah?	An elderly person unable to fast, or a sick person with no hope of recovery, gives for each missed day the amount needed to feed one poor person.
When is sadaqat al-fitr given?	It is wajib. It is best given before the Eid al-Fitr prayer.
To whom is zakat given?	To the eight groups listed in Surah at-Tawbah, verse 60: the poor, the needy, zakat collectors, those whose hearts are to be reconciled, slaves, debtors, those in the cause of Allah and travellers.
To whom may zakat not be given?	Not to ancestors such as parents and grandparents, not to descendants such as children and grandchildren, not to one's spouse, and not to the wealthy.
What are the obligatory acts of Hajj?	Ihram, standing at Arafat, and the tawaf of visitation (tawaf al-ifadah).
When and how is the sacrifice (qurban) made?	It is made during the first three days of Eid al-Adha. Sheep and goats must be one year old, cattle two and camels five. Up to seven people may share one large animal.
What are the conditions of repentance?	Feeling remorse, abandoning the sin at once, resolving never to return to it; and if another person's right is involved, settling it with them.
What is backbiting (ghibah)?	Mentioning someone in their absence in a way they would dislike. If what is said is false, it is slander.
What is needed for a marriage contract (nikah)?	Mutual offer and acceptance, at least two witnesses and a dowry (mahr).
How is istikhara performed?	Two rak'ahs of voluntary prayer are prayed, followed by the supplication of istikhara.
When is the Duha (forenoon) prayer prayed?	After the sun has risen about a spear's length until shortly before noon, between two and twelve rak'ahs.
What is the Sunnah?	The example set by our Prophet in worship and conduct. What he did consistently is called sunnah mu'akkadah (emphasised sunnah).
What is the difference between haram and makruh?	Haram is forbidden by definitive proof; one who does it sins. Makruh is a lighter prohibition than haram; one should avoid it.
What is najasah (impurity)?	Substances regarded as impure in religion, such as blood, urine, faeces and wine. For prayer, the body, clothing and place of prayer must be free of them.
How is wudu performed?	Make the intention and say Bismillah. Wash the hands, rinse the mouth and nose; then wash the face and arms (with the elbows), wipe the head and wash the feet (with the ankles).
What are the sunnahs of wudu?	Intention, Bismillah, washing the hands, using a miswak, rinsing the mouth and nose, washing three times, keeping the order and wiping the ears.
May one touch the Quran without wudu?	According to the Hanafi school, the mushaf may not be touched without wudu. Reciting from memory is permissible.
What must a person in a state of janabah avoid?	Until performing ghusl, they may not pray, circumambulate the Kaaba, recite the Quran, touch the mushaf or enter a mosque.
What is the qibla?	The direction of the Kaaba. Facing the qibla is a condition of prayer.
What are the adhan and iqamah?	The adhan announces that the prayer time has begun; the iqamah is recited when starting the obligatory prayer. Both are emphasised sunnah for the five daily prayers.
When does the Fajr time begin?	It begins with true dawn (the whitening of the horizon) and lasts until sunrise.
When is Dhuhr prayed?	It begins when the sun passes its zenith towards the west and lasts until Asr time.
When is the Maghrib time?	It begins at sunset and lasts until Isha time begins.
How many rak'ahs are the daily sunnah prayers?	The 2 before Fajr, the first 4 and last 2 of Dhuhr, the 2 of Maghrib and the last 2 of Isha are emphasised sunnah. The first 4 of Asr and of Isha are non-emphasised sunnah.
Why is the sunnah of Fajr important?	It is the strongest of the sunnah prayers. Our Prophet never abandoned it.
What is tahiyyat al-masjid?	Two rak'ahs of voluntary prayer offered on entering a mosque before sitting down.
How is the Friday prayer performed?	It is two obligatory rak'ahs, prayed with a sermon (khutbah) and in congregation, and it replaces Dhuhr.
What should be known about the Eid prayer?	It is a wajib prayer of two rak'ahs offered on Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha. The sermon is given after the prayer.
What is the takbir al-tashriq?	The takbir said after the obligatory prayers from Fajr on the Day of Arafah until Asr on the fourth day of Eid.
When is the sacrifice made?	After the Eid prayer, from the first day of Eid until the evening of the third day.
For whom is the sacrifice wajib?	For residents who own wealth reaching the nisab beyond their basic needs.
What is aqiqah?	An animal sacrificed in gratitude for a newborn child. It is good to do it on the seventh day after birth.
When is zakat given?	When one year has passed over wealth reaching the nisab, zakat becomes due.
On which kinds of wealth is zakat due?	Gold, silver and money, trade goods, and livestock meeting certain conditions.
How is zakat on trade goods calculated?	If at the end of the year the value of trade goods reaches the nisab, one fortieth (2.5%) of the value is given as zakat.
What is ushr?	The zakat on agricultural produce: one tenth for crops watered by rain, one twentieth for crops irrigated at a cost.
How is the intention for fasting made?	Intention is the resolve of the heart. Saying it aloud is not required.
What should be known about iftar and suhur?	Getting up for suhur and breaking the fast on time, without delay, are sunnah.
Which are the voluntary fasts?	Such as Mondays and Thursdays, the 13th, 14th and 15th of each month, the day of Ashura and six days in Shawwal.
On which days is fasting forbidden?	Fasting on the first day of Eid al-Fitr and on the days of Eid al-Adha is forbidden.
Does a menstruating or postnatal woman fast?	She does not fast in this state. The missed days of Ramadan are made up later.
What if a pregnant or nursing woman does not fast?	If she or her baby would be harmed, she may refrain from fasting and make it up later.
Must a traveller fast?	A traveller may refrain from fasting during the journey and make up the missed days later.
What is i'tikaf?	Staying in a mosque with the intention of worship during the last ten days of Ramadan.
Which night is Laylat al-Qadr?	It is one of the last ten nights of Ramadan; it is most likely held to be the 27th night.
What types of Hajj are there?	Three: ifrad, tamattu' and qiran.
What are the wajib acts of Hajj?	Acts such as standing at Muzdalifah, stoning the pillars, sa'y, shaving or cutting the hair and the farewell tawaf.
For whom is Hajj obligatory?	Once in a lifetime for a Muslim who is sane, mature, free and physically and financially able.
What is Umrah?	Worship performed by entering ihram, circumambulating the Kaaba, performing sa'y and shaving or cutting the hair. In the Hanafi school it is an emphasised sunnah.
How is the deceased prepared?	The body is washed, shrouded, the funeral prayer is offered and it is buried.
How many pieces does the shroud have?	Three for men, five for women.
What is the etiquette of reciting the Quran?	If possible, being in wudu, facing the qibla, beginning with the ta'awwudh and basmala, and reciting slowly and clearly.
How many surahs does the Quran have?	114 surahs and 30 juz'. The first surah is al-Fatihah, the last an-Nas. The first verses revealed were the first five verses of Surah al-Alaq.
What is the prostration of recitation (sajdat at-tilawah)?	A prostration made when a verse of prostration is recited or heard. In the Hanafi school there are fourteen such verses and the prostration is wajib.
What does sending salawat mean?	Sending blessings and peace upon our Prophet with respect and prayer. It is commanded in Surah al-Ahzab, verse 56.
What are the key dates in our Prophet's life?	He was born around 571, received the first revelation in 610, emigrated in 622, conquered Mecca in 630 and passed away in 632.
What are the attributes of the prophets?	Truthfulness (sidq), trustworthiness (amanah), intelligence (fatanah), infallibility (ismah) and conveying the message (tabligh).
Who are the Ulu'l-Azm prophets?	Noah, Abraham, Moses, Jesus and Muhammad.
Who are the four great angels?	Jibril (Gabriel), Mika'il (Michael), Israfil and Azra'il.
Which are the four great books?	The Torah, the Psalms (Zabur), the Gospel and the Holy Quran.
What are qada and qadar?	Qadar is Allah's eternal knowledge and decree. Qada is the creation of what was decreed when its time comes. Man is responsible through his free will.
What is shirk?	Associating partners with Allah. The Quran states that the shirk of one who dies without repenting will not be forgiven.
What are the rights of others (kul hakkı)?	Taking another person's right or harming them. Such a wrong is forgiven only if the wronged person forgives it.
How is du'a made?	Begin with praise of Allah and salawat on our Prophet, ask sincerely and persistently, and end with Amin.
What is istighfar?	Asking Allah for forgiveness. Our Prophet sought forgiveness a hundred times a day.
What is the Hijri calendar?	A lunar calendar starting from our Prophet's emigration (Hijra). It was introduced in the time of Umar. A year has about 354 days.
Which are the sacred months?	Dhu al-Qa'dah, Dhu al-Hijjah, Muharram and Rajab.
Which are the blessed nights (kandil)?	Ragha'ib (the eve of the first Friday of Rajab), Mi'raj (27 Rajab), Bara'ah (15 Sha'ban) and Mawlid (12 Rabi' al-Awwal).
What is good to do on Friday?	Performing ghusl, wearing clean clothes, applying perfume, going early to the mosque and sending much salawat.
What are the sunnahs of Eid?	Performing ghusl, wearing one's best clothes, saying takbir, attending the Eid prayer and visiting relatives and neighbours.
What should be known about giving and returning salam?	Giving salam is sunnah; returning it is obligatory.
What is said after sneezing?	The one who sneezes says "Alhamdulillah". The one who hears says "Yarhamukallah", and the sneezer replies "Yahdikumullah".
What is the etiquette of eating?	Wash the hands, begin with Bismillah, eat with the right hand and from what is in front of you, avoid waste and praise Allah at the end.
What is done when entering and leaving the mosque?	Enter with the right foot and leave with the left. A supplication is said when entering and leaving.
What should be known about interest (riba)?	Interest is forbidden. Surah al-Baqarah, verse 275, states that interest has been made unlawful.
What should be known about alcohol and gambling?	Alcoholic drinks and gambling are forbidden. Surah al-Ma'idah, verse 90, commands us to keep away from them.
Which foods are forbidden?	Carrion, flowing blood, pork and animals slaughtered in a name other than Allah's are forbidden. (al-Ma'idah, 3)
How is halal slaughter done?	By saying Bismillah, with a sharp instrument, cutting the animal's throat (windpipe and gullet).
What is mahr?	Property the man must give the woman upon marriage. It is one of the rulings of the marriage contract.
What is the expiation for a broken oath?	For a broken oath one must feed or clothe ten poor people. One who cannot afford it fasts three consecutive days.
What is a vow (nadhr)?	Making a non-obligatory act of worship binding on oneself. Fulfilling it is wajib.
What is our responsibility towards our parents?	Treating them kindly, not hurting them and obeying them in matters that are not sinful. It is commanded in Surah al-Isra, verse 23.
