```
[Dev] I built a free prayer times app focused on remembering our departed – feedback welcome

Assalamu alaykum! I made Secdem, a free Android app:
- Accurate prayer times (Diyanet, district level) with countdown, adhan alert, widget
- Qibla compass, tasbih, full Quran with verse-by-verse audio
- Grave records: save loved ones (it can read names/dates from a gravestone photo, on-device) and get a Friday reminder to recite Fatiha and Ya-Sin for them
- Nearby mosques, zakat calculator, kids' corner with a quiz
- English, Turkish, Arabic, German

No account needed; data stays on the device. Ad-supported.
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nurvakti.namazvekuran
I'd love feedback, especially from Muslims living in Europe.
```
